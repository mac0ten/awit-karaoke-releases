Keypad maker

Make the keys yours: a shape, liquid glass, colours and places. Copy the style, then paste it in AWIT Karaoke.

Tap a key to colour it.

1Shape

Each corner, from square to fully round.

2Colours

Tap a key on the keypad to colour it. The keys you leave alone take the colours of the theme picked in the app.

3Places

Tap Move keys, then two keys to swap them. The numbers, CAN, RSV and MENU stay where they are.

4Copy it

  1. Tap Copy style.
  2. In AWIT Karaoke open Settings › App theme (Simple mode: Settings › Theme).
  3. Under Keypad, tap Paste a style. Your keys change at once.
